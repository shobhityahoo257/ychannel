Turn a news story into a Hindi video in a few clicks
⚠️ First add your OpenAI key in the Settings tab to make real videos. (Without a key you can only make a demo video.)

3. Your photos (optional)

Add real photos. The AI decides which photo goes where and keeps faces in frame. If you add too few, stock photos fill the gaps (needs a Pexels key).

📷 Drag photos here or click to choose
Photos you upload are saved to your library automatically, and the AI may also pick relevant ones from it.

4. Speech / video clips (optional)

To include a politician's real statement, provide the clip yourself (Sansad TV, PIB, or your own recording). The app never downloads copyrighted video from the internet.

Clip rules
• Every clip needs a Source; "Source: ..." is shown on the video.
• At most 30 seconds are used from any one clip, and clips can be at most 40% of the whole video.
• Write times like "0:12" or "1:05". Leave empty to start from the beginning.
