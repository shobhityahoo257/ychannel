4. Speech / video clips (optional)

To include a politician's real statement, provide the clip yourself (Sansad TV, PIB, or your own recording). The app never downloads copyrighted video from the internet.

Clip rules
• Every clip needs a Source; "Source: ..." is shown on the video.
• At most 30 seconds are used from any one clip, and clips can be at most 40% of the whole video.
• Write times like "0:12" or "1:05". Leave empty to start from the beginning.
